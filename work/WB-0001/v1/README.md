# A short proof of Deligne's bound for Frobenius eigenvalues, after Alpöge

WB-0001, version 1: [hexagon:2611.00789](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00789), deposited 21 November 2026.

L. Alpöge sketched, in a footnote of their note of 20 November 2026 on the Riemann hypothesis, a proof of the theorems
of Weil and Deligne that uses only a sign. The paper writes that argument out in full: Deligne's bound for the
eigenvalues of Frobenius, which contains the Riemann hypothesis for varieties over finite fields. The ideas are those
of the sketch. The paper supplies the reduction to the lowest cohomology group of a smooth affine variety, the finite
map that makes the count a character sum, the weak Lefschetz theorem behind the sign (Deligne's, in the appendix of
N. Katz's *Affine cohomological transforms, perversity, and monodromy*), and the passage to all degrees.

The proof was produced by GPT-6 Astra (ChatGPT Pro mode) from the sketch and checked by Claude Opus 5.5 at effort high
and, independently, by Claude Fable 5.2 at effort max, which wrote the published text. No human has checked it.
